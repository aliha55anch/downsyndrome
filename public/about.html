About Down Syndrome

Clear and simple answers for parents and families

This page gives general information, not medical advice. Please talk to a qualified doctor about your child.

What is Down syndrome?

Down syndrome is a genetic condition. A person with Down syndrome is born with an extra copy of chromosome 21. This changes how the body and brain develop.

Children with Down syndrome learn, grow and make progress. They often learn a little more slowly, so early support at home helps a lot. Every child is different, with their own personality, strengths and dreams.

What causes it?

The extra chromosome happens by chance when a baby begins to form. It is nobody's fault. It is not caused by anything a parent did or did not do.

Everyday Care

Health and care

Regular checkups

Children with Down syndrome may need extra checks, such as heart, hearing, eyesight and thyroid. Ask your doctor which checks your child needs and when.

Healthy food

Give fruit, vegetables, protein and water. Some babies need help with feeding, so ask a doctor or therapist for advice.

School and learning

Many children do well in schools that welcome all children. Special educators can help plan simple goals for your child.

Family support

You are not alone. Talk to other parents, join support groups and ask for help when you need it.
